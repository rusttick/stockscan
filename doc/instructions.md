
at my machine shop I have shelves full of a variety of tool steel stock.
It is neatly arranged on the shelves with all pieces next to each other.
it is not stacked vertically.
all pieces have one end aligned with the front of the shelf.
the ends of the stock are color coded.
I need to inventory the dimensions of each piece of stock metal to roughly 0.1in for the 2 dimensions easily visible from the end of the shelf
and around 0.25in precision for the length of each piece.


I would like to develop a computer vision process to record the measurements of every piece.
The system does not need to differentiate between materials or alloys or anything like that.
The alloys are physically segregated onto different shelves and labeled.

I want the daily inventory process to go from manually measuring and accounting for each piece
to a process where someone can take a series of photographs and upload them to a system
that will extract the inventory and compare it to yesterday's inventory data.

The measuring device is limited to a smart phone camera.
theoretically we could clip on a polarizing filter if specularity becomes a problem.
